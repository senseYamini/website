Research Focus

Doctoral research on RF-based sensing with emphasis on Wi-Fi sensing spanning three core directions:

(i) Power-efficient Wi-Fi localization for resource-constrained IoT devices, addressing battery consumption in background location services

(ii) Network-efficient Wi-Fi sensing for distributed systems, solving scalability bottlenecks in multi-sensor deployments

(iii) Adversarial Wi-Fi sensing and defenses against privacy attacks, protecting RF-based systems from eavesdropping and inference attacks

Key Projects

Power-Efficient Wi-Fi Sensing
Engineered two systems (LowFI and LiteTrack) for power-aware positioning using Sionna RT-based channel modeling and motion-triggered scanning. Achieved 10–22× battery savings over continuous scanning while maintaining >80% classification accuracy across 22 locations and 10+ devices.
Network-Efficient Wi-Fi Sensing
Developed two-stage framework combining lightweight occupancy detection and SSPOC-based compression. Achieved >4000× compression with 85% accuracy, 35.5% throughput improvement, and >90% latency reduction in distributed sensing systems.
Adversarial Wi-Fi Sensing & Defense
Formulated black-box defense as constrained optimization balancing SNR preservation with adversarial misclassification. Degraded adversarial localization accuracy from 98% to 17% while maintaining signal quality. First to address SNR-communication tradeoffs in ISAC security.
